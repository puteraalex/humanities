# Sources and assumptions
Every real data source, derived value and game-rule assumption is listed here.
“Item costs, masses and effects (src/data/items.json), the budget of 100 and the mass limit of 40 are game assumptions, not real spacecraft specs.”
“Storm timing, strength and the three response options (src/data/events.json) are game rules. Real storm data and sources come in a later milestone.”
## Real source
- NASA/JPL release 2024-080 (June 10, 2024): the May 20, 2024 solar storm at Mars. Curiosity RAD measured about 8,100 micrograys, compared by NASA to 30 chest X-rays. Archived, not live.
## Game rules
- The 30-point burst, radiation points, shield percentages, sheltering effects, all costs, masses and meters are invented for play.
